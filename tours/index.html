A starting point, not a fixed package

3 relaxed days in Da Nang & Hoi An

Day 1 · Coast & city

Ease into Da Nang

Start with a beach walk, choose a local lunch spot and spend the evening by the Han River. Keep your arrival day flexible.

Day 2 · Choose an adventure

Mountains or heritage

Choose a full day at Ba Na Hills, or a shorter Marble Mountains visit with a relaxed afternoon. Allow time for transfers and weather changes.

Day 3 · Streets & flavours

Slow down in Hoi An

Explore the old town, try a local dish and stay for the lantern-lit riverside. Arrange your return transport before the evening.

Adjust the route for your arrival time, mobility needs and weather. Meals, tickets and transport are booked separately.
